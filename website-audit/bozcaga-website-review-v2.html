Website Review — Tuğba Bozçağa (https://tugbabozcaga.github.io/)

Prepared in advance of the Oxford CASSM/DPIR–DSPI Associate Professorship shortlist meeting. Covers all six pages (About, Research, Teaching, Geospatial Analysis in R, Other, Vita).

This version replaces the v1 review. Two factual claims in v1 have been corrected: (i) the Springer chapter _12 URL is the correct chapter for “Syrian Refugee Integration in Turkey,” verified against Springer; the v1 claim that _25 was correct was wrong. (ii) Development Analytics is dual-located — UK Companies House registers the company at 9 Marlborough Court, Oxford OX2 0QT, and the institute also lists an Istanbul address. Calling “Oxford-based” a flat factual error was wrong; the issue is that the description undersells DSPI-relevant substance, not that the geography is misstated.

The full website-vs-application inconsistency inventory (54 items) and the per-link verification table (~95 instances) are retained as Appendix A and Appendix B at the end of this document — useful for whoever does implementation but not the lead deliverable.

1. Overall read

The site is competent and content-rich, and lands reasonably on substance — the AJPS placement, prize density, and methodological seriousness all show. But the moment a reader starts checking, three things go wrong at once.

Link integrity is broken in visible places. The flagship AJPS paper “Paper” link goes to a different paper (the Mozambique paper), and the Mozambique paper link goes to the AJPS paper — the two SSRN IDs are swapped. Eight separate links route to empty Wix template pages. Three MPSA award links return 404. Two co-author links (Harding, Kutlu) are broken. The Google Scholar link in every page header is still user=YOUR_ID. Imams and Patrons is listed as “forthcoming” but has been published in JCR since September 2025.

The site is a stale subset of the application document. The 2024 Oxford DPIR Visiting Scholar position, four-doctoral-student supervision, KCL Performance Recognition Award, MZES Mannheim 2026 workshop, Oxford DPIR/DSPI/MEC seminar talks, AALIMS Faculty Fellow status, APSA PE Council Member role, and KCL institutional service are all on the application CV and absent from the website. Several places where the website contradicts the application: AALIMS Faculty Fellow vs. site’s “Junior Fellow”; APSA Council Member vs. site’s generic “member”; three Mozambique co-authors in the application vs. two on the site.

Framing under-sells DSPI. The site reads as “political economy of development.” The application Research Statement reframes it as “political economy of development with an emphasis on service provision and social welfare” — exactly the DSPI register — and reorders the pillars. None of that has been ported.

Single most important fix: unswap the two SSRN links, replace the eight Wix stubs, fix the Scholar placeholder, and reconcile AALIMS / APSA PE / Visiting Scholar / supervision against the application CV. Then port the Research-Statement opening to the About page so the site reads DSPI-legible at first skim.
